# HomeVerse Product Overview

HomeVerse (家庭管理平台) is a modern family digital management platform that creates a dedicated digital universe for each family. It unifies precious memories, important documents, and shared information in one place.

## Core Purpose

Centralized family life management including:
- Photo albums and file storage
- Shared calendars with lunar calendar support
- Financial tracking and budgeting
- Recipe management and meal planning
- Health records and growth tracking
- Todo lists and chore management
- Shopping lists and contacts
- Wiki knowledge base
- Password vault with encryption
- AI-powered content generation and image/video tools

## Key Features

- Multi-user family system with 5 role types (Owner, Admin, Editor, Viewer, Member)
- Fine-grained permission control
- Real-time notifications via WebSocket
- Global search across all content types
- Activity logging for audit trails
- Multi-provider AI integration (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Qwen, Wavespeed)
- MinIO object storage for files and photos
- PWA support for mobile installation

## Target Users

Families looking to organize their digital life, share memories, coordinate activities, and maintain important records in a private, secure platform.
